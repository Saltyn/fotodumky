The FotoDumky Standard
What we mean when we say "real photography."
Real Capture

Every image begins as light hitting a sensor, not a prompt typed into a machine. If it was not photographed, it does not appear here.

No Generative Manipulation

What the camera saw is what you see. Nothing was added, removed or generated after the shutter closed, aside from the FotoDumky logo.

Human Authorship

A person stood somewhere, at some hour, and decided this moment was worth keeping. That decision, and the eye behind it, cannot be automated.

Provenance

Every published work is dated and catalogued, and can be checked against its original file on request. Authenticity can also be independently verified using tools such as ExifTool.

Transparency

When a tool assists presentation, such as the optional room visualization, we say so, clearly, every time. Real is not a marketing word here. It is a constraint we accept.

This is not a badge, and nobody has to take our word for it.
It is a standard we hold ourselves to, checkable on request, before anyone asks.
